Zoro

Zoro is a personal AI assistant used by the Zoro NutriDiet team on its own computer. It helps the team research evidence-based nutrition science and prepare and publish educational content on the team's own YouTube channel and social media pages.

Zoro is not offered to the public. It only acts on accounts owned by the Zoro NutriDiet team.

Zoro uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's videos to the owner's YouTube channel and to read that channel's analytics. Use of Zoro is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
