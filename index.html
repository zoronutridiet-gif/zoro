Zoro

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Zoro is my private desktop assistant. I am Dr. Srinivas Patel Punyala, and I run the Zoro NutriDiet YouTube channel. Zoro runs on my own Windows laptop. It helps me research nutrition science and make and publish evidence-based videos on my own channel.

I am the only person who uses Zoro. It is not offered to anyone else.

Zoro uses YouTube API Services. It uploads videos to my own channel only through the official YouTube Data API, reads my own channel's analytics and the comments on my own videos, and reads public YouTube data for research. Use of Zoro is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

In chat and reports, data that Zoro gets from YouTube API Services is processed by open models hosted by Groq. The privacy policy explains how this data is used, stored and deleted.

Zoro's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. To remove Zoro's access to a Google account, visit security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
